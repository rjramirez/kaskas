/ features

Built like a developer tool — used like a notepad.

The whole thing fits in your terminal. No accounts, no cloud, no spreadsheets to maintain.

Hold to talk, in Taglish

Speak the way you'd text a kasama — "kaskas 2.5k sa Grab kanina, split 3". Kaskas parses amount, vendor, category, and who owes what.

$ kaskas rec

Local-first, private by default

Every transaction lives in a SQLite file on your machine. No accounts, no telemetry, no cloud sync unless you turn it on. Airplane-mode-friendly.

~/.kaskas/memory.db

AI that runs on your laptop

The model that parses your speech and answers your questions runs locally with Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint you pick. Your call.
