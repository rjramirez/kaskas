# Pinoy Expressions for KasKas

**Note:** Taglish mode is optional. Enable with `taglish: true` in config or per-command.

---

## Greetings & Reactions

| Context | Taglish Expression | When Use |
|---------|-------------------|----------|
| Good savings | "Ang tipid mo, beh!" | Low spending detected |
| Very good savings | "Sulit na sulit!" | Under budget |
| Overspending | "Hala, lumobo na gastos mo!" | Over budget |
| Danger zone | "Lagot ka na, beh!" | High utilization |
| Due reminder | "Uy, malapit na due date mo!" | 3 days before due |
| Overdue | "Grabe, late ka na sa bayad!" | Past due date |
| Cashback earned | "Swak! May cashback ka!" | Cashback detected |
| High utilization | "Ingat, mataas na ang utang mo!" | >60% utilization |
| Subscription found | "May nakita akong subscription, baka di mo na ginagamit?" | Unused sub detected |
| Payday | "Sweldo day! Wag kalimutan mag-ipon!" | 15th or 30th |
| Big purchase | "Malaki yan ah, sure ka ba?" | Large transaction |
| Installment | "Hulugan mo na lang para di masakit sa bulsa" | Installment suggestion |
| Success | "Ayos! Tapos na!" | Task complete |
| Error | "Ay, may problema!" | Error occurred |

## Encouragement

| Situation | Taglish Expression |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Paid on time | "Galing! On time ka sa bayad!" |
| Reduced spending | "Nice, bumaba gastos mo!" |
| Cancelled unused sub | "Tama yan, tipid is life!" |
| Low utilization | "Safe ka pa, beh!" |
| Emergency fund growing | "Ang laki na ng ipon mo!" |

## Warnings

| Situation | Taglish Expression |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Near limit | "Malapit ka na sa limit, ingat!" |
| Multiple dues | "Dami mo babayaran this week ah!" |
| Spending spike | "Ang taas ng gastos mo ngayong buwan!" |
| Subscription creep | "Dami mo na subscription, beh!" |
| Impulse buy pattern | "Parang ang dami mong bili lately ah" |

## Common Phrases

- "Beh" = friend/buddy (gender-neutral, casual)
- "Sulit" = worth it
- "Tipid" = frugal/savings
- "Gastos" = expenses
- "Ipon" = savings
- "Utang" = debt
- "Bayad" = payment
- "Sweldo" = salary
- "Hulog" = installment
- "Libre" = free / to treat someone

## Usage

### Default (English)
```
High utilization detected (65%). Reduce spending.
```

### Taglish Mode
```
Mataas na utilization mo (65%), beh! Ingat sa gastos.
```

### Enable Taglish
- Config: `"taglish": true`
- Per-command: `/review --taglish`
- Chat: "Use Taglish mode"
