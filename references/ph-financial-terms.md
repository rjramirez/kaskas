# Philippine Financial Terms

## Common Pinoy Money Terms

| Pinoy Term | English | Context | Example |
|------------|---------|---------|---------|
| Sweldo | Salary/Wage | Payday tracking | "Kailan sweldo mo?" |
| Sahod | Salary (alt) | Same as sweldo | "Dumating na sahod ko" |
| Utang | Debt/Loan | Obligation tracking | "May utang ako sa kanya" |
| Hulog | Installment | Payment plans | "Monthly hulog ko sa kotse" |
| Pautang | To lend | Lending money | "Pwede pautang?" |
| Bayad | Payment | Bill payment | "Bayad na ako sa kuryente" |
| Singil | To collect/bill | Collection | "Siningil na ako ng landlord" |
| Ipon | Savings | Savings tracking | "Mag-ipon ka na!" |
| Alkansya | Piggy bank | Coin savings | "Lagay mo sa alkansya" |
| Gastos | Expenses | Spending | "Ang laki ng gastos ko" |
| Puhunan | Capital | Business investment | "Kulang pa puhunan ko" |
| Kita | Earnings/Profit | Income | "Magkano kita mo?" |
| Tubo | Interest/Profit | Returns | "Malaki tubo sa negosyo" |
| Patong | Markup/Interest | Added cost | "May patong yan" |
| Paluwagan | Rotating savings | Group savings | "Kasali ako sa paluwagan" |
| Remittance | OFW money | Family support | "Dumating na remittance" |
| Padala | Send money | Remittance | "Nagpadala na si tatay" |
| Aguinaldo | Christmas bonus | 13th month | "Malaki aguinaldo ko!" |
| Suki | Regular customer | Loyalty | "Suki na ako dyan" |
| Tawad | Bargain/Discount | Haggling | "Pwede tawad?" |
| Libre | Free/To treat | Treating others | "Libre kita!" |
| Tingi | Retail/Small qty | Small purchases | "Tingi lang bili ko" |
| Buo | Wholesale/Whole | Bulk purchase | "Buo na lang bilhin mo" |
| Piso | Peso | Currency | "Magkano sa piso?" |
| Sentimo | Centavo | Small change | "Wala akong sentimo" |

## Credit Terms

| Term | Meaning | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|
| Credit limit | Maximum borrowing | "Lumagpas sa limit" |
| Available credit | Remaining limit | Limit minus balance |
| Minimum due | Minimum payment | Avoid late fees |
| Statement date | Billing cycle end | Monthly statement |
| Due date | Payment deadline | Usually 21 days after statement |
| Finance charge | Interest | If not paid in full |
| Annual fee | Yearly card fee | Some cards waived |
| Cash advance | ATM withdrawal | High interest! |
| SIP | Straight installment | 0% installment |
| Balance transfer | Move debt | Lower interest promo |
