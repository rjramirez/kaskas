# Philippine Financial Calendar

## Paydays (Sweldo Days)

| Day | Type | Notes |
|-----|------|-------|
| 15th | Mid-month | First sweldo |
| 30th/31st | End-month | Second sweldo |
| Last Friday | Some companies | Weekly/bi-weekly |

**Pattern**: High spending 1-3 days after sweldo. Track for "sweldo syndrome."

## Ber Months Warning (Sep-Dec)

Filipino spending pattern:
- September: Christmas decorations start
- October: Gift planning begins
- November: Early shopping, 11.11 sales
- December: Peak spending month

**13th Month Pay**: Usually released mid-December. Expect big purchases.

## Remittance Patterns (OFW)

Common remittance days:
- 1st of month
- 15th of month
- Before major holidays
- Before school opening (June, August)

**Note**: Track for family budget planning
