## Tips

### Maximize Rewards
1. Match card to spending category
2. Stack with e-wallet cashback
3. Use during bank promo days
4. Pay in full to avoid interest
5. Track points expiration

### Avoid Fees
- Pay before due date
- Stay under 30% utilization
- Request annual fee waiver
- Avoid cash advances

### E-Wallet Strategy
- GCash: Bills, GCredit
- Maya: High-interest savings
- ShopeePay: Shopee purchases
- GrabPay: Grab services
