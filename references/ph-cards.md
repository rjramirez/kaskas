# Philippine Credit Card Reward Patterns

**IMPORTANT:** Rates and rewards change frequently. Verify with issuer before deciding.
Data based on historical patterns. No live promotions included.

---

## HSBC Gold
- **Issuer**: HSBC
- **Primary Focus**: Dining & Entertainment
- **Reward Type**: Cashback
- **Known Rewards**:
  - Dining: 5-10% cashback
  - Groceries: 2-3% cashback
  - Other: 1% cashback
- **Best For**: Frequent diners, entertainment spending

## BPI Amore
- **Issuer**: BPI
- **Primary Focus**: Groceries & Supermarkets
- **Reward Type**: Cashback
- **Known Rewards**:
  - Groceries: 5% cashback
  - Dining: 2% cashback
  - Other: 1% cashback
- **Best For**: Grocery shoppers, household spending

## UnionBank Platinum
- **Issuer**: UnionBank
- **Primary Focus**: Online Shopping
- **Reward Type**: Cashback/Points
- **Known Rewards**:
  - Online: 3-5% cashback
  - Dining: 2% cashback
  - Other: 1% cashback
- **Best For**: E-commerce, online purchases

## BDO Gold Mastercard
- **Issuer**: BDO
- **Primary Focus**: Installment Promos
- **Reward Type**: Installment flexibility
- **Known Rewards**:
  - 0% installment on select merchants
  - Flexible payment terms
  - Installment promos frequent
- **Best For**: Large purchases, installment buyers

## Metrobank M Liter
- **Issuer**: Metrobank
- **Primary Focus**: Fuel & Transport
- **Reward Type**: Cashback
- **Known Rewards**:
  - Fuel: 5% cashback
  - Transport: 2% cashback
  - Other: 1% cashback
- **Best For**: Drivers, commuters

## Security Bank Platinum
- **Issuer**: Security Bank
- **Primary Focus**: Travel & Entertainment
- **Reward Type**: Points/Miles
- **Known Rewards**:
  - Travel: 2-3 points per PHP
  - Dining: 2 points per PHP
  - Other: 1 point per PHP
- **Best For**: Travelers, frequent flyers

## PNB Visa Signature
- **Issuer**: PNB
- **Primary Focus**: General spending
- **Reward Type**: Points
- **Known Rewards**:
  - All categories: 1 point per PHP
  - Bonus points on select merchants
- **Best For**: Balanced spending

## Citibank Rewards
- **Issuer**: Citibank
- **Primary Focus**: Flexible rewards
- **Reward Type**: Points (convertible)
- **Known Rewards**:
  - All categories: 1 point per PHP
  - Points convertible to cashback/travel
- **Best For**: Flexible reward redemption
